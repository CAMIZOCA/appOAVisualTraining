• Vibración y sonido opcionales.

PRIVACIDAD
• Sin cuentas, sin anuncios y sin rastreo.
• Todo se guarda únicamente en tu dispositivo.

## English (U.S.)

**Name:** OA Visual Training

**Subtitle:** Guided visual exercises

**Promotional text:**
Voice-guided visual activities to train and rest your eyes in a few minutes. No accounts, no ads. Made by Óptica Andina.

**Keywords:**
eyes,vision,eye exercises,eye strain,eye break,optometry,tracking,saccades,focus,screen,relax

**Description:**
OA Visual Training is the Óptica Andina app with guided visual activities to train and rest your eyes in short sessions.

Pick an activity, follow the on-screen target and let the voice guide you step by step.

ACTIVITIES
• Tracking: horizontal, vertical, circles, star, figure eight and H pattern.
• Saccades: two-point jumps and random jumps.
• Near / far focus.
• Guided convergence.
• Visual break and blink reset to rest from screens.

EASY TO USE
• Voice instructions in Spanish and English.
• Sessions of 1 to 3 minutes.
• Adjustable speed, target size and target type.
• High contrast and reduced motion.
• Optional haptics and sound.

YOUR PROGRESS
• Session history, minutes practiced and day streak.
• A recommended activity every day.
• Log how you felt after each session.

PRIVACY
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
• Everything is stored only on your device.

IMPORTANT NOTICE
This app does not diagnose, treat or cure visual conditions and does not replace professional ophthalmological or optometric care. Stop the activity if you feel pain, persistent double vision, dizziness or discomfort, and consult an eye care professional.

## App Review notes

```
OA Visual Training offers guided visual wellness activities (a moving on-screen target
with voice instructions). It is not a medical device: it does not diagnose, treat or
measure anything, and it shows a safety notice on first launch.

- No account or sign-in is required. All features are available immediately.
- No data is collected. Settings and session history are stored only on the device.
- Internal tool disclosure: the app includes a "Developer / test mode" used by our
  optometrists to tune activity parameters (speed, target size, voice texts). It is
  hidden by default. To enable it: Settings > About > tap "Version 1.0.0" seven times.
  A "Developer / test mode" section then appears in Settings. It does not unlock any
  paid content or change the app's purpose.
```
